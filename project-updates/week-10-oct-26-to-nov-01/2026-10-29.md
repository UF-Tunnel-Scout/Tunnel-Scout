# Thursday, Oct 29

The flight controller arrived and the frame was assembled with motors. Roberto bought the remaining parts, inspected the fiber converters, and stored everything in the team's lab locker. Stakeholder meeting with the TA, 11–12.

## Hope

- Saw the flight controller had come in and went to pick it up (10:50 AM).
- Confirmed the team locker number for parts storage (5:19 PM).

## Alex

- Posted a battery option, the Zeee 3S 5200 mAh (11:39 AM, #parts_list). Roberto pointed out the drone needs 6S.

## Roberto

- Arrived 10:52 AM for the stakeholder meeting (11–12).
- Researched the MicoAir743 V2 FC and ESC stack (12:06 PM).
- Mounted the motors on the frame (1:00–1:09 PM).
- The CNHL battery couldn't be ordered, so he compared replacements (11:58 AM – 1:54 PM):
  - Flywoo Explorer 6S Li-ion 4500 mAh ($49.99, about 19–24 min): first choice
  - Auline 6S 3300 mAh LiPo ($74.49, about 12–15 min): backup
- Bought the Pi Active Cooler and accessories (2:06–2:48 PM). Found a microSD card the team already had, so it no longer needed buying (3:08 PM).
- Opened and inspected the fiber media converters and their SFP modules (3:24 PM).
- Bought the shortest Ethernet patch cables he could find (4:10 PM).
- Checked the Pi 5 with the Perma-Proto HAT (4:43 PM).
- Stored the parts in the lab locker (5:35 PM).
- Reminded everyone to sign the cave waiver and read the preserve's checklist (8:13 PM).

## Problems / what's next

- 6S only: the 3S pack won't lift the drone. Try the Flywoo Li-ion first, with the Auline LiPo as backup.
- "Everything is bought," apart from small parts later (4:27 PM).
