## Gabriel

- Brought the Xbox controller to the lab (said he'd arrive about 12:12 PM).
- Debugged the 5 V UBEC and checked the battery on the bench with Claude, using the Rigol DM3068 meter (12:27–1:26 PM):
  - The probes had been on the voltage-select header, not the OUT pads. Measure at OUT+/OUT−.
  - The "0.15 A" reading was really 0.15 mA of divider current. Load current needs the meter in series.
  - Confirmed the pack is a CNHL 6S 3700 mAh (25.2 V full), just inside the UBEC's input limit. Never charge it in LiHV mode.
  - Caught the meter still in current mode with the red lead in the 10 A jack before probing the battery, which would short the pack.

## Decisions / blockers

- **The BEC failed during the motor test.** Probable cause: input voltage spikes above its 26 V limit (full 6S is 25.2 V).
  The replacement Matek BEC12S-PRO (9–55 V, built-in TVS) is due Nov 12.
- Gabriel's chat identified the board as a "Henge 8A UBEC" (7–25.5 V). Roberto's notes call it the RMRC 8A UBEC. The records don't say whether these are the same board under two names, so check the label.
- The pack on the bench is 3700 mAh, not the 3300 mAh on the parts list.
