## Gabriel

- Planned the motor checks with Claude (11:17 AM): hand-spin and multimeter checks, then a current-limited first power-up with props off.
  Noted the three parts still pending: fiber converter, RPLIDAR C1 and smoke stopper.
- Reviewed the full wiring diagram (11:28 AM). Flagged Pi 5 power through the GPIO 5 V pin and failsafe on fiber loss as the two biggest risks.
- Laid out the build order based on Oscar Liang's 7-inch build, and what can be soldered now vs later (11:31–11:40 AM).
  Found the stack ships with a 470 µF cap, not the 1000 µF in the diagram (11:52 AM).
- Started a step-by-step Pi 5 bench bring-up (11:33 AM). Step 1: fitting the Active Cooler.
- Prepped the ESC soldering (11:53 AM – 12:49 PM): 60/40 rosin-core solder; iron at 680–700 °F for motor pads and 750–790 °F for battery and capacitor pads;
  motors first. Flagged checking the motor KV (1300 KV for 6S) and the ESC pad order vs ArduPilot's Quad X.
- Said he'd be back the next day to finish soldering the motors (6:30 PM).
