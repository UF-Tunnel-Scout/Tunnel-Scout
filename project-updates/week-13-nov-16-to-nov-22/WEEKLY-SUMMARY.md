# Week 13 (Nov 16 – 22), so far

- **Tuesday:** a flight test had torn the heat shrink on the battery's positive lead, so Gabriel put new heat shrink over it. He also soldered the BEC's 5.2 V output to a USB-C cable for the Pi and heat shrunk the joints, so the Pi can now run off the drone's battery through the BEC. See [Gabriel's log](2026-11-17_gabriel-daily-log.md).
- **Wednesday:** Gabriel added a second BEC output to power the Ethernet modem that will connect the fiber optic line. Small probe spots on the leads were used to confirm the Arduino and modem both got the right voltage and powered on. After that, all the wires were cut to their final mounting lengths, re-soldered and heat shrunk, and the finished BEC was soldered to the drone at the battery connection. See [Gabriel's log](2026-11-18_gabriel-daily-log.md).

**Next up:** meter both BEC outputs off the drone's battery, then run the Arduino and modem together and watch for brownouts. Mount the modem, Arduino and BEC on the frame and bring up the fiber link.
