- Blockers: the replacement BEC is due Nov 12. Communication tests need the new Pi OS, which Alex is setting up.
- Plan: Thu Nov 12, set up the Tango in QGC, write the new FC settings, and run the motor-order and throttle-only tests. Fri Nov 13, fly in AltHold.
